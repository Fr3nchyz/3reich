# Our map compared with the original game's map data

Roadmap item 2.4. The original 1996 program keeps its map in `BINMAP.BIN`: one record per hex with its nation, front, terrain code, city flags, and which of its six hexsides are water, crossable by land, crossing arrows or rivers (format in [GAME_DATA.md](GAME_DATA.md)). `scripts/gamedata/map.ts` decodes it and lists every field where `src/data/map.json` disagrees. This page records what the comparison found, what was corrected, and what is left as a proposal. The game files stay outside the repository; everything here is hex ids and our own words.

A correction was made only when a second, independent signal agreed with the game: the measurement of the reference-map scan in `scripts/map/extract.py`, or what the scan plainly shows when the hex or hexside is looked at (every corrected item was checked by eye on a crop of the scan). All corrections are curated overrides in `scripts/map/curated.py`, each with a comment citing `BINMAP.BIN`, and `src/data/map.json` was regenerated by `scripts/map/extract.py` (the run is reproducible: two runs give identical files, and the unchanged generator reproduced the previous `map.json` byte for byte before any edit).

## The open questions

**Coastal slivers and the 4% land threshold.** The game's land hexes are exactly the hexes with any land on the scan: every land hex in the game measures at least 0.4% land, and every all-sea hex in the game at most 0.3% (those are the green US Box dots). So the game settles the land or sea status of every hex on the map, including every hex that depends on the threshold:
- S42, V39, H26, J29, EE19, KK25, CC28, DD19 and K25 (Great Yarmouth), which the threshold kept as land: land in the game too. Confirmed, no change.
- 19 hexes with 0.4% to 4.0% land that the threshold made all sea are land in the game. 17 are now land with the game's country: B33, G31, G38, H23, H37, I22, I34, T40, U49, W52, Z48, BB25, BB48, DD29 (Chios), EE27, FF32, LL20. W21 (Corsica) and Z18 (Sardinia) are proposed, not made, because they would change the `corsica` and `sardinia` zones in `src/data/zones.ts` (see below).

The 4% threshold stays in `extract.py` as the measurement step, but it no longer decides any hex on its own: with the overrides, our land and sea status matches the game on every hex except W21 and Z18. Retiring it in code would need those two proposals applied and a land test of "any land at all" (0.35%) whose margin (0.3% against 0.4%) is too thin to trust on a JPEG scan.

**Sicily and Sardinia.** In the game all of Sicily is joined by land: DD20-DD21, DD19-EE19 and EE19-EE20, which our map called coastline only, are crossable. Corrected (DD20-DD21 is plain land on both sides on the scan; the other two join the sliver hexes to the same island). Sicily's only link to the mainland is the Messina crossing arrow DD21-DD22, as on our map; DD22 belongs to the mainland in the game's area table. Sardinia: Z18 is land in the game, joined by land to Z19 and AA18, and Z19-AA19 is crossable in the game but not on ours; both are proposals because Z18 would join the `sardinia` zone. Corsica: W21 is land in the game, joined to X20 (proposal, `corsica` zone).

**Beaches.** The game marks 55 beach hexes, ours had 36. 35 of ours agree. Corrected: F40, H34, L21, L22, T10, U19, Y38, Y39, EE21, EE32 and LL18 are now beaches (the tan strip is visible on the scan; H34, U19 and EE32 also became coastal, as the game says, with 0.7% to 2.6% water on the scan), and JJ23 is no longer one (no tan strip on the scan). Proposed: D35, K30, V35, W43, CC26, DD3, GG36, KK33, LL19, where either the scan shows no water inside the hex or the strip cannot be told from desert colour.

**Borders.** Two country errors, both corrected. D43 is Finnish in the game; on the scan its strip of north shore lies west of the drawn Finland-USSR border (the border runs along C44-D44). CC30 (Lesbos) is Greek in the game and labelled "(Gr)" on the scan; our map had joined it to Turkey through a false land hexside, BB31-CC30. The national boundaries then follow from the countries and the land hexsides; the two left (J35-J36, U26-V26) come from proposed land hexsides.

## Proposed, not made

- **W21 and Z18 land**, with W21-X20, Z18-Z19 and Z18-AA18 crossable. Needs `src/data/zones.ts` changed in the same step: `corsica` becomes X20 and W21, `sardinia` gains Z18 (T01 owns that file).
- **Tirane is a city, not a capital** in the game, and on the scan it is a black dot under "Albania (It)". Not made because the automatic setup ranks capitals first and `tests/setup.test.ts` (also edited by T01) uses Tirane as the hex the 1-3 goes to.
- **Hexsides crossable by land in the game only** where the coast reaches just one end of the hexside on the scan: A40-B39, A41-B41, A48-B48, D42-D43, G25-H24, G33-H33, J35-J36, L21-L22, L22-L23, M21-N20, R43-S42, S41-S42, U26-V26, V35-W35, V38-V39, W53-X52, Y8-Z7, Y38-Y39, Z19-AA19, AA32-AA33, BB6-BB7, FF33-FF34, LL19-MM19, LL27-LL28, LL28-LL29. The game seems to allow a land crossing wherever land touches both sides anywhere along the hexside; our map asks for more. No scan measure separates the two readings cleanly, so these wait for a rule decision (Ref 4.3).
- **K32-K33** (Stettin-Kolberg): blocked in the game, land on both sides on the scan. Unclear.
- **Coastal status**: 55 land hexes have water hexsides in the game but less than 4% water on the scan (many have none at all, the coast running along the hex edge). F41 and FF39 hold lake water on the scan but no water hexside in the game.
- **Terrain**: E25 is mountain in the game (a sliver of Kintyre); X45 is mountain in the game but plain on the scan, X48 the reverse (left as on the scan); the game also gives MM26, MM27 and NN25 the Qattara code, while the manual names only NN26's hexsides (Ref 4.51), which already agree.
- **Cities**: the game has a city at CC46 (a dot on the scan, name unreadable) and capital flags on R20 (Vichy, which becomes a capital in play) and A66 (a hexrow-label hex at the map's corner). AA21, open sea west of Naples, carries city and port flags. Left as they are.
- **Missing hexes**: CC47, EE46 and GG45 are playable Persian land hexes in the game, joined by land to our map; our extractor skipped them (CC47 holds a printed hexrow number). Adding hexes needs a curated "force playable" option in `extract.py`.
- **Rivers**: 45 of ours are not rivers in the game and 7 of the game's are not on ours. Many of ours lie on coasts, lakes or the Suez Canal; not checked one by one.
- **Water hexsides**: the game marks 306 more hexsides as water than our map's sea hexsides, most of them between coastal hexes, and marks 53 from one side only. Our sea hexside means "blue on both sides, fleets may cross"; what the game's water bit means for fleets is not settled, so nothing was changed. The four sea hexsides the game does not have (B44-C44, F41-F42, G41-H41, EE30-EE31) are lake or narrow-water sides.

## Impact on PR #20

PR #20 (branch `t01-zones`, at commit 007e8f2) passes all 270 of its tests with the old `map.json`. With this branch's `map.json` copied in, the full suite gives 267 passed and 3 failed, all in `tests/gamedata.test.ts`, all because the map now agrees with the game where it used to differ. Whoever merges second updates them as follows:

1. **"zones from the original game's files > marks a few hexes our map calls sea".** It expects zone hexes that are not land on our map: LL20 (1942 Libya west), U49, W52, Z48 (1942 U.S.S.R. east), T40 (1942 U.S.S.R. west), BB25 (1944 Italy south), T40, U49, W52, Z48 (1944 U.S.S.R. east), G38 and H37 (Eastern Europe). All of these are now land with the matching country, so the list is empty: expect `{}` (or drop the test).
2. **"zones from the original game's files > leave only D43 (both years) and V39 (1944) of the U.S.S.R. outside both start-line zones".** D43 is now Finnish (BINMAP.BIN nation 10, and west of the drawn border on the scan), so it is no longer U.S.S.R. land: expect `[]` for 1942 and `["V39"]` for 1944, and rename the test.
3. **"zones read off the map > the Baltic, North Sea and Atlantic ports are those on waters reached from the Atlantic without a Mediterranean hex".** Bilbao (S15) is now a city, not a port (black dot on the scan, city in the game), so `baltic-north-atlantic-ports` in `src/data/zones.ts` must drop S15.

Nothing else on that branch fails: the zone sizes and the territory checks there now follow the map, and the new land hexes fall inside the zones the game's bitmaps give.
