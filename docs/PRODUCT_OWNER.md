# How you can help (no coding needed)

You are the product owner: you decide what "right" looks like. The goal is a faithful recreation of the classic DOS game your dad played, modelled on the physical board game, with clean modern 2D visuals (readable and close to the real board and counters, nothing heavy). Here is where your judgement helps most, from quickest to biggest.

## 1. Try what exists (about 10 minutes, any time)

Open the site (3reich.amaurymarque.com on a computer or iPad), pick a scenario and a side, and set up your forces by tapping.
- Does anything confuse you? Which words or buttons are unclear?
- Does tapping feel right on the iPad? Are the targets big enough?
- Take screenshots of anything odd and send them with one line on what you expected.
- Try 1942 and 1944: they are new and have never been looked at by a person.

## 2. Ask your dad (the most valuable thing)

He played the original. Four questions from the earlier message, still open: which device, which scenario he'd start with, which side he plays, and what annoyed him in the original. Add: *what did he like about the screen*, and *does he remember a moment that felt wrong or unfair* (rules he disliked). His answers decide what we build first.

## 3. Rule questions only a person with the rulebook or the box can answer

If you or your dad own the Third Reich manuals or the board game, these are the open questions (plain language):
- Who controls the Greek island marked EE27 at the start of 1942 and 1944?
- In 1942, must the Soviets put six ground factors next to Leningrad and six next to Moscow, or six in total?
- May Germany start with more than one armor and one infantry in Libya (1942)?
- Why is the Crimea hex V39 left out in 1944?

(Web research already settled: the manual citations Ref 21.51 and 20.44, and that Vichy and Free French fleets get the same naval modifier as France. See `docs/RULES.md`, "Web research".)
Even "I can't find it" is useful to know.

## 4. Photos and screenshots that settle things

- Close-up photos of the physical counters, the map, and the charts (your eBay photo already helped with the coasts). Counters at a size where numbers and symbols are readable.
- If you can run the original under Boxer on your Mac: screenshots of specific screens: the setup screen with red lines, the Tables menu, a combat result, the status screen. They become our reference for "does ours match".

## 5. Look and feel: decisions I need from you

Right now the map is flat coloured hexes and the counters are plain labelled rectangles; it works but does not look like the board game. Pick a direction (I can mock up 2 or 3 to choose from):
- **Map:** a paper-style map with softer colours and a printed-map feel, or flat and clean.
- **Counters:** cardboard-style squares with the printed number layout of the real counters, or modern simple tokens. National colours only, no emblems.
- **Readability first:** larger type, strong contrast, zoom that is easy with a thumb.
- **Dad's comfort:** big buttons? fewer screens? a "what can I do now?" hint? Say what he'd want.

## 6. Decisions only you can make

- **Legal note:** the game's program file was analysed (never copied) to settle three rules. Keep that approach, or keep it out of the project? The README legal line must then be updated. Is the repo staying private?
- **Order of work:** my plan is rules first (economy, movement, combat), then the computer opponent, then the 1939 game against the computer as the first release. Anything your dad would rather have earlier?
- **Specs:** five short documents describe what each part must do (`docs/specs/`). Read any one and say "let's go", or tell me what is wrong. Nothing in those parts is built until you do.
- **Language:** English first; French later as planned.
- **Review standard:** reviews are currently done by the same model family. If you want a human second opinion on the map or rules, someone with the physical game is ideal.

## A good first week

1. Try 1939, 1942, 1944 on your iPad and send screenshots.
2. Send your dad the four questions.
3. Read spec 01 and spec 04 (turn order and combat) and say go or change.
4. Pick a visual direction from my mockups.
