# Rulings of 2026-10-29 (owner-approved rules research)

On 2026-10-29 the owner (Amaury) approved every recommendation of the rules-research findings page (Gemini and Copilot reports compared with the repo's PC manual audit). **Neither AI report could be checked against the manual from the research session**, so every row below is "resolved by agreement of sources", not "read in the manual". Copilot cites Ref and Ops rule numbers, Peele's errata and the v1.38 patch notes; Gemini is mostly board-game recall. Where a row says DOSBox it stays an open `TODO(verify)`.

A ruling changes no code by itself. The last column says whether the engine already matches. Rows that need engine work have a ticket (see "Tickets"). Engine work that moves whole-game results merges one PR at a time and reports `npm run compare-games`; nothing that moves many games merges before the owner has seen the numbers.

## Copilot's airbase rules with no usable Capital (Q2, Ref 20.1, Ops 5.48h)

- The U.S.A. airbase goes to the U.S. Box.
- If Moscow is lost, a Soviet airbase goes to a hex on the Russian east edge.
- An enemy-held Capital eliminates the airbase.
- A full Capital does not eliminate it: the excess air units move to the nearest base.
- The "return to the reserve" idea has no source and is dropped.

Engine today: the U.S. airbase is eliminated (A3) and a Soviet one tries Moscow (A2); differs. Issue #187. Still DOSBox: an airbase whose Capital is enemy-held, and one whose Capital is full.
